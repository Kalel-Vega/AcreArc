⚠️ Honest limitations

  • 9 km resolution. Satellite readings (e.g. SMAP soil moisture) average a ~9 km × 9 km cell. Your paddock can differ a lot — hill vs. flat, sandy rise vs. heavy clay swale.
  • Simulated ensemble. The "100 trees" are a lightweight in-browser simulation of a Random Forest for demonstration; they show uncertainty, not a guarantee.
  • Sample data. This demo uses built-in illustrative values, and prices/yields are simplified "wheat-equivalent" estimates.
  • Crops modelled. Wheat, Barley, Canola, Chickpea, Lupin and Forage Sorghum, plus Field Pea as a break crop in Option 3.
  • Your decision. AcreArc supports, never replaces, your experience and your agronomist. No data leaves your device.
